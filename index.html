The base rates (Budget ₹2,000, Comfort ₹4,400, Luxury ₹9,600 per person per day) are built from listed prices on hotel and houseboat booking sites, checked every few months. Your actual trip can run 10-20% higher or lower depending on which hotels you pick and how far in advance you book.

December and January are Kerala's peak season: houseboats and beach-facing rooms in Alappuzha, Kumarakom, and Varkala routinely sell out weeks ahead, and prices follow demand. We apply a 1.25x multiplier for those two months. June to August is the monsoon, with fewer visitors and around 15% lower prices, though some houseboat routes reduce sailings in heavy rain.
