About

We built the planner we wished existed

Keralam Travels is a small, India-based site focused on one thing: giving Kerala-bound travelers a realistic budget before they start booking.

Why we made this

Most Kerala trip content is either a glossy brochure or a forum thread with numbers from three years ago. Neither tells you what a specific trip, your dates, your travelers, your style, will actually cost. The Trip Budget Planner exists to close that gap: three base rates, a seasonal adjustment, and a day-by-day layout you can build in under a minute.

How we keep the numbers current

We check listed prices on major hotel and houseboat booking platforms every few months and adjust the base rates if they've moved meaningfully. We do not track every hotel in Kerala, and we say so plainly: this is a planning estimate, not a live price feed.

How we make money

The planner is free and always will be. Some of the outbound links under your plan, to hotel and houseboat search pages, are affiliate links. If you book through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That is the entire business model.

What we are not

We are not a travel agency and we do not hold inventory. We do not call ourselves Kerala experts with decades of local guiding experience, because that would not be true; we are a planning tool built by people who travel in Kerala regularly and check the numbers.
