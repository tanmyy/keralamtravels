Estimates, not quotes

Every number produced by the Trip Budget Planner is an estimate based on category-level pricing, not a confirmed quote from any hotel, houseboat operator, or transport provider. Actual prices vary by date, availability, and how far in advance you book.

We are not a travel agency

Keralam Travels does not sell travel packages, hold hotel inventory, or process bookings. Booking links in the planner go to third-party sites; any transaction happens between you and that provider under their terms.

Affiliate relationships

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. We may earn a commission if you book through them, at no extra cost to you. This does not affect which destinations or routes we cover.

Accuracy

We check base rates periodically but cannot guarantee they reflect current market prices at every moment. Seasonal events, festivals, and sudden demand spikes can move prices faster than we update.

Travel conditions

Road conditions, weather (particularly during the June to August monsoon), and wildlife park closures can affect itineraries shown on this site. Confirm current conditions with your accommodation or a local operator before finalizing plans.
