Munnar to Alleppey, 5 Days

Two nights in the tea hills, then three days winding down onto the backwaters. This is the single most common first-trip route in Kerala, and for good reason: it covers hills and water without too much driving.

Day 1-2, Munnar

  • Arrive from Kochi (about 4 hours by road), settle in, evening at leisure
  • Tea estate walk and museum, Top Station viewpoint at sunset
  • Spice garden visit and, if the weather holds, a short trek toward Kolukkumalai

Day 3, Transfer

  • Drive from Munnar to Alleppey (around 5 hours), stopping at a waterfall en route

Day 4-5, Alleppey

  • Board a houseboat by early afternoon, cruise through the backwaters overnight
  • Morning canoe ride through the narrower canals, village walk, disembark by 9am
