Wayanad for Trekkers, 4 Days

Four days built around trails, not sightseeing loops. Wayanad's high plateau stays cool enough to trek most of the year, and the reward-to-effort ratio is the best in Kerala.

Day 1, Kalpetta and Edakkal Caves

  • Arrive Kalpetta from Calicut airport (about 3 hours by road), settle into a homestay
  • Afternoon climb to Edakkal Caves for the stone-age petroglyphs; the 45-minute ascent is a good warm-up for Chembra
  • Dinner in Kalpetta town, early night

Day 2, Chembra Peak

  • Start early with a packed breakfast; guided trek to the heart-shaped lake and the summit, 4 to 5 hours round trip
  • Forest department permits are capped each day, so weekdays are easier than weekends
  • Rest in the afternoon, plantation dinner in the evening

Day 3, Safari and Banasura

  • Morning wildlife safari at Muthanga or Tholpetty, best between 7 and 11am: elephants, deer, peacocks
  • Afternoon at Banasura Sagar dam, then a spice plantation walk at sunset

Day 4, Coffee estates, depart

  • Morning coffee plantation walk with a tasting, lunch in Kalpetta, drive back to Calicut for your flight
