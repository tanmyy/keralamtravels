Kumarakom Long Weekend, 3 Days

Kumarakom is the backwater you reach in a morning and leave reluctantly. Three days is the minimum that lets you slow down to lake pace.

Day 1, Arrival

  • Arrive from Kochi (about 2 hours by road), check into a lake-facing homestay
  • Evening walk along the Vembanad bund road at sunset, dinner of karimeen (pearl spot fish)

Day 2, Birds and the houseboat

  • Dawn visit to the Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, best from November to February
  • Board a day houseboat by 11am for a cruise on Vembanad; disembark around 5pm

Day 3, Villages and a massage

  • Morning cycling through paddy fields and coconut groves, afternoon Ayurvedic massage
  • Depart for Kochi in the evening
