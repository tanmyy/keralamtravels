Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

The Trip Budget Planner runs entirely in your browser. Your destinations, dates, traveler count, and add-on choices are never sent to our servers; they exist only on your device while you use the planner. We do not require an account or email address to use any part of this site.

Analytics

We use standard, privacy-respecting web analytics to understand which pages get traffic and which itineraries people look at. This data is aggregated and not tied to your name or contact details.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies for basic site function. We do not run third-party ad tracking on this site.

Third-party links

Booking links on this site (to hotel and houseboat search pages) take you to third-party sites with their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for how those sites handle your data.

Contact

This site does not collect personal data, and there is no contact inbox. The planner runs entirely in your browser.
