What to Pack for Munnar, by Season

Munnar surprises first-timers because it does not feel like Kerala at all. Pack for the altitude and for your season, and the tea hills are easy. Pack for the beach and you will be cold.

The basics, any season

Layers beat bulk: a fleece or light jacket for evenings, even in March. Trekking shoes with real grip, because estate trails turn muddy after even light rain. A small daypack, a reusable water bottle, and a headlamp or torch if you are doing any sunrise viewpoint trips.

June to August (monsoon)

A rain jacket or poncho, not an umbrella: the wind on viewpoints makes umbrellas useless. Waterproof shoe covers or sandals you do not mind soaking, a dry bag for your phone and camera, and extra socks. Trails close and reopen with the rain, so keep your plans flexible.

December to February (winter)

A proper warm jacket. Mornings can drop toward 5 degrees, and the wind at Top Station cuts through a thin fleece. Gloves and a beanie if you plan a sunrise trek, plus lip balm and moisturizer: the air is dry.

March to May and September to November

Sun protection first: cap, sunglasses, sunscreen. The sun at 1,600 meters burns faster than on the coast. Light layers for the evening, when the temperature drops quickly after sunset.

Honest caveat: Munnar town has shops for anything you forgot, but trekking shoes and decent rain gear cost more there than at home. Pack those two; buy the rest locally if needed.
