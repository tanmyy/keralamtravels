Self-Drive Car Rental in Kerala: Cost, Deposit and Rules

Self-drive car rental in Kerala starts at about Rs 1,200 to Rs 1,800 a day for a hatchback in Kochi, Rs 1,800 to Rs 2,500 for a sedan and Rs 2,500 to Rs 3,800 for an SUV, checked in October 2026 on a major aggregator. Most operators also take a refundable deposit of Rs 5,000 to Rs 10,000, fuel is extra, and you need a valid driving licence held for at least 1 year. Foreign visitors also need an International Driving Permit with their home licence and passport.

Last updated: October 2026

Self-drive works best when your trip links places that taxis price as separate one-way drops: Kochi to Munnar at about 130 km, Alleppey at about 50 km, Thekkady at about 185 km and Wayanad at about 270 km from Kochi. For a single short hop, a prepaid taxi or bus is simpler. This guide uses Kochi prices, where most visitors pick up, including near Cochin International Airport. See also our guide to reaching Munnar from Kochi airport.

What documents and licence do you need?

DriverDocuments operators ask for
Indian residentValid original driving licence for a light motor vehicle or 4-wheeler, plus Aadhaar card or another government ID such as passport, voter ID or PAN card
Foreign visitorHome country driving licence plus a valid International Driving Permit (IDP), plus passport. Carry the IDP and the home licence together: the IDP is not a standalone licence
Additional driverSame licence and ID checks, named on the booking. EVM Wheels lists an additional driver charge and includes an additional driver on its Premium package

Get the IDP in your home country before travel. It is valid for up to 1 year and is expected alongside the home licence at rental and police checks. Travellers staying longer than 1 year are expected to apply for an Indian licence.

Plan kilometres honestly: Kochi to Munnar and back is about 260 km before sightseeing, so a 135 km per day package needs at least 2 days for that loop without extra charges.

How to book a self-drive car in Kerala, step by step

  1. Pick the car for the route, not just the price. Hatchback for city and coast, sedan for flat family runs, SUV for Munnar, Thekkady and Wayanad ghat sections.
  2. Enter pickup city, dates and drop point. Kochi city, Kakkanad, Aluva and the airport are common pickup areas. Confirm if delivery is included or charged extra.
  3. Compare the full package. Daily rate, kilometres included, extra kilometre charge, deposit, fuel policy, cancellation terms and permitted travel area, all for the same dates.
  4. Complete document verification. Show your licence and ID, and complete Aadhaar OTP verification before pickup where the aggregator asks for it. Foreign drivers should flag their IDP status at booking.
  5. Pay, then inspect. At handover, video the car, note existing damage in writing, and confirm fuel level, FASTag and return time.
  6. Return on time. Keep the return inspection receipt until the deposit is released.

Self-drive or a taxi with a driver: which is cheaper?

For a single Kochi to Munnar leg, a one-way sedan taxi starts at about Rs 3,200, as in Kochi to Munnar taxi fare vs bus cost. Self-drive competes when you keep the car for 3 or more days and visit two or three bases, instead of paying for several one-way drops. It loses for one short transfer, such as Kochi airport to Fort Kochi, or if you do not want to drive ghat roads. Add fuel, tolls and extra kilometres before deciding.

Driving note: start ghat climbs in daylight, go slow on wet hairpins, and never drive outside the permitted area or let an unnamed driver drive: deposit and insurance terms are tied to named drivers.

FAQ

Can foreign tourists rent a self-drive car in Kerala?

Yes, with a home licence, a valid International Driving Permit obtained before travel, and a passport. Carry both licence documents together.

What is the minimum age for self-drive rental in Kerala?

21 years on most standard cars. Premium SUVs can require 25 years and 5 years of driving experience, and luxury cars 28 years and 8 years, as EVM Wheels lists. Check the category of the exact car you want.

Is fuel included in the rental price?

Usually no. Most Kochi listings exclude fuel and use a like-for-like or full tank to full tank policy. Tolls, parking, fines and interstate taxes are also extra.

Can I take a self-drive car from Kochi to Munnar or Wayanad?

Yes: Munnar is about 130 km and Wayanad about 270 km from Kochi. Choose a kilometre package for the full loop, and confirm hill use is permitted.

Can I drop the car in a different city?

Only with select partners, usually for a surcharge. Confirm Kochi to Trivandrum one-way availability and the surcharge at booking, not at pickup.

What happens if I exceed the kilometre limit or return late?

Extra kilometres are billed per kilometre, and late returns attract additional charges. Both can be recovered from the deposit, along with damage and fuel shortfalls.

Honest caveat: prices, deposits and kilometre packages were checked in October 2026 from Zymo, RenTrip, IndusGo and EVM Wheels listings and terms. They vary by model, dates and package. Some sites advertise zero deposit or unlimited kilometres on specific plans while standard terms differ, so rely on your booking page, confirm inclusions in writing, and cost fuel and tolls on top in the trip budget planner.
