Kerala Trip Cost for a Family of 4 for 5 Days

A self-planned 5-day Kerala trip for a family of 4 costs roughly Rs.65,000 to Rs.1,10,000 at mid-range, excluding flights to and from Kerala. That covers two hotel rooms a night, an Ertiga or Innova with a driver for the full five days, a 2-bedroom houseboat for one night with all meals, food for four, and the main activities. Budget families can do it for Rs.45,000 to Rs.65,000, and a premium trip starts around Rs.1,30,000. The numbers below are per-day line items built from verified 2026 operator rates.

The short answer: what 5 days in Kerala costs a family of 4

These are self-planned totals for 4 nights and 5 days, for two adults and two children, excluding flights or trains to Kerala. They assume the classic route: Kochi to Munnar (2 nights), Thekkady (1 night), and an Alleppey houseboat night (1 night) before heading home from Kochi.

Where the money goes: the three family multipliers

Three things make a family trip cost more than a couple's trip, and understanding them is how you control the budget.

1. Two rooms, every night. A family of 4 with two children usually needs two double rooms: most hotel double rooms are built for two adults plus one small child. At mid-range, that means Rs.3,500 to Rs.4,500 per room per night at a decent 3-star property, so Rs.7,000 to Rs.9,000 a night for the family. Some properties offer connecting rooms or a family suite for about 1.5x a double room's price, which beats two separate rooms if you can find it. Budget homestays drop the per-room rate to Rs.1,800 to Rs.2,500, which is the single fastest way to cut the total.

2. The cab. A sedan does not seat four plus luggage comfortably, so families hire an Ertiga or an Innova for the full trip. Verified 2026 rates: Ertiga from Rs.2,500 to Rs.4,500 a day and Innova (manual) from Rs.3,500 to Rs.6,500 a day, with driver bata, tolls, and parking added on top of most base quotes. Budget Rs.3,000 to Rs.5,000 a day for the family for five days. The Innova's comfort is real on the Munnar ghat roads, but the Ertiga is 30 to 40 percent cheaper and seats six, so most families of 4 can manage with it if they pack light. Our Kochi to Munnar taxi vs bus guide breaks down how cab choice moves the total.

3. The houseboat night. This is the biggest single day of the trip and the one worth getting right. A 1-bedroom boat is sized for two adults, so a family of 4 needs a 2-bedroom boat. Based on verified 2026 Alleppey rates, a 2-bedroom premium boat with all meals lands around Rs.12,000 to Rs.21,000: start from the 1-bedroom premium rate (Rs.9,000 to Rs.18,000) and add the standard extra-person charge of about Rs.1,500 each for the additional family members, meals included. Deluxe 2-bedroom boats run Rs.10,000 to Rs.15,000 on the same logic. All rates carry 5% GST on top, and October to March is peak season, so expect quotes toward the higher end of these ranges in December and January. See our full Alleppey houseboat cost guide for deluxe vs premium vs luxury and what each tier actually includes.

Honest caveat: houseboat quotes are where families get burned. Confirm in writing the boat category, the number of bedrooms, AC hours (Silver-category boats sometimes run AC only from 9 PM to 6 AM), and what happens if you want an extra hour of cruising. A quote that seems cheap usually has an asterisk on one of these.

A sample 5-day route that keeps the driving sane

Day 1: Land in Kochi, easy evening at Fort Kochi (no long drive after the flight). Day 2: Drive to Munnar, about 4 hours, stopping at Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls. Day 3: Eravikulam National Park (entry about Rs.100 per Indian adult, children less) and the Tata Tea Museum. Day 4: Drive to Thekkady; Periyar sanctuary entry (Rs.33 per Indian adult) plus the forest-department boat safari (Rs.225 per adult, Rs.75 for children 5 to 11 at the counter, 2-hour ride), and a spice plantation walk. Day 5: Drive to Alleppey, board the houseboat by noon, cruise the backwaters, disembark next morning for the flight or train home. This mirrors our 5-day Munnar to Alleppey itinerary, which is the same skeleton a family budget should follow.

Keep the driving honest: Kochi to Munnar is about 130 km, Munnar to Thekkady about 85 km, and Thekkady to Alleppey about 140 km. Resist adding Kovalam or Wayanad to a 5-day family trip; the extra 200 km turns the trip into a driving schedule the kids will remember for the wrong reasons.

What changes with kids in tow

Kerala is kind to families: Periyar boat tickets are Rs.75 for children aged 5 to 11, sanctuary and park entry fees are half or less for children, and most mid-range hotels add a child's breakfast free. Two things to price properly: first, houseboat extra-person charges (about Rs.1,000 to Rs.1,500 each with meals in deluxe and premium, more in luxury), which apply to older children; second, the cab: children count as passengers for seating, so four passengers means a 6 or 7-seater, not a sedan with someone squeezed in. Houseboats are safe for families with kids in the normal sense: calm canals, life jackets on board, and no open sea. Keep small children supervised on the sundeck and away from the gangway at check-in and checkout, when the boat is closest to the jetty.

Five ways to cut the bill without cutting the trip

One, travel in October or early November instead of December: the weather is already good and houseboats and hotels sit below their December-January peaks. Two, take the Ertiga over the Innova and save 30 to 40 percent on the cab, the third-largest expense. Three, book a family suite or two rooms in a homestay instead of two rooms in a 3-star; homestay kitchens also cut breakfast and dinner costs. Four, if the houseboat quote shocks you, do a day cruise or shared shikara in Alleppey (Rs.600 to Rs.800 per person on shared boats) plus a canalside hotel night, instead of the overnight boat. Five, skip the "private" upsells at the jetty and the commission-driven shopping stops your driver suggests; both add to the bill without adding to the trip. FAQ

How much does a 5-day Kerala trip cost for a family of 4?

Rs.65,000 to Rs.1,10,000 at mid-range, excluding flights, for a self-planned trip: two hotel rooms for 3 nights, a 2-bedroom houseboat for 1 night with all meals, an Ertiga or Innova with a driver for 5 days, mid-range food, and the main activities. Budget families can manage Rs.45,000 to Rs.65,000; premium runs Rs.1,30,000 and up.

Is one hotel room enough for a family of 4?

Usually not. A standard double room fits two adults plus one small child at most. Budget for two rooms a night, or ask for a family suite, which typically costs about 1.5 times a double room and often works out cheaper than two rooms.

Ertiga or Innova for a family of 4 in Kerala?

The Ertiga seats six and costs Rs.2,500 to Rs.4,500 a day, while the Innova costs Rs.3,500 to Rs.6,500 a day. The Innova is noticeably more comfortable on the Munnar ghat roads, but the Ertiga handles a family of 4 fine if you pack light and saves 30 to 40 percent on the trip's third-largest expense.

Is a houseboat safe for families with kids?

Yes. Backwater canals are calm, life jackets are on board, and crews are used to families. Watch small children on the sundeck and at the gangway during boarding. Budget extra-person charges of about Rs.1,000 to Rs.1,500 each with meals in the deluxe and premium categories.

What is the best month for a family trip to Kerala?

October to March is the main season: dry and cool. October to November and February to March are the best value windows, with good weather below peak prices; December and January are the most expensive months.

Is 2 days enough for Alleppey?

For most families, one night is the sweet spot: board at noon, cruise the afternoon, sleep on the boat, disembark in the morning. A second day repeats the same canals. If the family wants more water time, add a morning shikara ride instead of a second houseboat night.
