How Much Does a Private Houseboat in Alleppey Cost for 2 People for 1 Night?

A private 1-bedroom houseboat in Alleppey costs roughly Rs.7,000 to Rs.13,000 per night for 2 people in the deluxe category, Rs.9,000 to Rs.18,000 in premium, and Rs.15,000 to Rs.30,000 in luxury, based on 2026 operator rates. Those are peak-season (October to March) prices; off-season (April to September) runs about 20 to 30 percent lower. The price covers the whole boat for just the two of you, all meals on board, and a cruise from 12 PM check-in to 9 AM checkout. GST at 5% is usually added on top.

Last updated: September 2026. Rates below are what Alleppey operators actually list in 2026, not estimates built from old blog posts.

2026 price table: private 1-bedroom houseboat for 2

Operators in Alleppey sell houseboats by category and season, not by a fixed price list. A 1-bedroom private boat is what nearly all couples book: the whole boat is yours, one private bedroom with an attached bathroom, a dining deck, and a crew. Here is what that costs in 2026, per night, for 2 people:

The wide bands are real: a boat's price depends on its condition, its age, its reviews, the exact dates, and how full the fleet is that week. Most operators charge GST at 5% on top of these base rates, so always confirm the all-in figure in writing before you pay a deposit.

Deluxe vs Premium vs Luxury: what the extra money buys

The category names sound like marketing, but the practical difference is mostly about one thing: air conditioning.

DeckOpen deckGlass-covered upper deckLuxury glass lounge, often with a private deck

The honest read: if you are cruising between October and February, a well-kept deluxe boat is perfectly comfortable, because evenings and nights are cool enough that daytime AC matters less. If you are going in the warmer months or the monsoon, when afternoons are humid, premium is worth the jump. The full-time AC and the glass-covered deck change the experience more than the fancier cushions do.

What is included in the price

Nearly every overnight houseboat in Alleppey is sold as an all-inclusive package. For the price above, you get:

  • A 21-hour cruise: check-in at 12 PM, checkout at 9 AM the next day, covering Vembanad Lake, the village canals, and Punnamada Lake.
  • All meals on board: a welcome drink, lunch while cruising, evening tea with snacks, dinner at the overnight mooring, and breakfast the next morning. Food is cooked on the boat by the crew, on a fixed Kerala menu.
  • Overnight accommodation with bedding, plus a captain and cook crew for the whole trip.

That fixed menu is why the price feels reasonable once you break it down: four cooked meals and a night in a floating room are built in.

What is not included, and what catches people out

  • GST at 5%, added on top of the quoted rate by most operators. Ask whether the quote is "plus GST" or "including GST."
  • Alcohol. You can usually bring your own; boats do not serve it.
  • Add-ons. Honeymoon decoration with a cake and flowers costs about Rs.1,500 to Rs.2,500 extra, arranged at booking time.
  • Extra guests. A 1-bedroom boat is sized for 2 adults. If a third person joins, operators charge roughly Rs.1,000 per extra person in deluxe, Rs.1,500 in premium, and Rs.2,500 to Rs.3,000 in luxury, with meals.

Season and holiday pricing: when a 1-night houseboat costs more

Three things push prices above the standard bands:

  • Peak season (October to March): full tariff. Book 3 to 4 weeks ahead for weekends.
  • The peak within the peak (about 20 December to 5 January): rates can hit 1.5x to 2x normal peak prices. This is the single most expensive window of the year, and boats sell out weeks in advance.
  • Festival dates: Onam, which fell on 28 August 2026, pushed rates to near-peak even though August is monsoon season. Vishu, Diwali, and Pongal weekends typically add 30 to 50 percent over the surrounding dates, and some operators quote up to double on the biggest holidays.

Weekend nights (Friday and Saturday) are usually 10 to 15 percent higher than weekday nights. If your dates are flexible, a mid-week boat in early October or late February gets you near-off-season pricing with peak-season weather. Off-season itself, April to September, runs about 20 to 30 percent below peak for the same boat.

How to pay less without getting a bad boat

  • Travel mid-week in the shoulder months. September, early October, and late February combine lower demand with good weather.
  • Consider a shared boat if privacy is not the point of your trip. Shared 1-bedroom boats start around Rs.7,500 per 2 people in deluxe and Rs.10,000 in premium on off-season lists, which undercuts most private boats.
  • Compare deluxe boats by condition, not just price. At Rs.7,000 to Rs.13,000 the spread is mostly about maintenance. Recent reviews with photos are a better guide than the category label.
  • Book direct with the operator and get the inclusions and the GST treatment in writing before paying a deposit. Agents add commission; direct quotes do not.

Frequently asked questions

Is the houseboat price per person or for the whole boat?

For the whole boat, privately. A 1-bedroom houseboat is yours alone whether you are a couple or booking it for one person, and the tables above are the full per-night price for 2 people.

What are the check-in and checkout times?

Standard check-in is 12 PM and checkout is 9 AM the next morning, about 21 hours on the water, with a short morning cruise before breakfast.

Do houseboats run during the monsoon?

Yes. The backwaters are calm inland waters, so normal rain does not stop the cruise; only very rare red-alert weather days cause disruption, and operators inform guests in advance. The monsoon (June to September) is also the cheapest season. See our Alleppey destination guide for what each season feels like.

Is a deluxe houseboat good enough for a honeymoon?

It can be, in the cooler months (October to February), when the night-only AC is not a problem. Most honeymooners book premium for the full-time AC and better interiors; the price gap over deluxe is usually a few thousand rupees per night. Luxury adds the glass lounge and a custom dinner, and makes sense mainly for a special occasion.

Honest caveat: operator rate lists move with demand, so treat every number above as a 2026 market band, not a quote. Confirm your boat's price, inclusions, and GST treatment in writing before paying.
