Varkala 2 Day Itinerary Budget

Two days in Varkala cost about Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,300 per person on a budget and Rs 2,850 to Rs 5,000 per person at mid-range for 1 night, excluding travel to Varkala. Day 1 covers the North Cliff, Papanasam Beach and Janardhana Swamy Temple. Day 2 covers Kappil Beach and backwaters, Edava and Anjengo Fort. Most sights are free, so your stay tier decides the total. How to split the 2 days

Stay near the North Cliff and walk to the beach, cafes and temple road. Keep Day 1 on foot, and save Kappil, Edava and Anjengo, which need an auto, for one loop on Day 2.

Varkala is the only place in southern Kerala where cliffs sit next to the Arabian Sea, according to Kerala Tourism. The cliff walk, views and sunset are free, and Papanasam Beach below is also free. You do not need ticketed activities to fill 2 days.

Day 1: North Cliff, Papanasam Beach and the temple

  1. Morning: Janardhana Swamy Temple and Papanasam Beach. Go early. This Vishnu shrine is described by Kerala Tourism as about two millennia old in origin, with the present structure from the 12th century. Entry is free. See our Kerala temple dress code guide first. Then head to Papanasam Beach and swim only where lifeguards allow it: the sea can be strong.
  2. Midday: Lunch and a slow cliff walk. Cliff cafes serve meals at about Rs 250 to Rs 600 per person in recent listings, and daily food at cliff cafes runs Rs 300 to Rs 700 per person. Eat the main meal at lunch, browse the shops, and keep the afternoon loose for the beach. Ask price and duration before any yoga or massage booking.
  3. Evening: Sunset from the North Cliff. Pick a cliff cafe for tea or dinner and watch the light change over the beach. Cafes often stay open until about 10 pm to 11 pm in season.

Day 2: Kappil, Edava, Sivagiri and Anjengo

  1. Morning: Kappil Beach and backwaters. Head north to Kappil, where the backwater meets the sea. Walk via Odayam and Edava if you have energy, or take an auto. Small boat rides here are listed at about Rs 400 to Rs 700 per boat, not per person, so confirm inclusions before you board. Kayak rentals, when operating, are about Rs 300 to Rs 600.
  2. Late morning: Sivagiri Mutt. Stop at Sivagiri, the hilltop mutt and final resting place of Sree Narayana Guru, about 3 km from town. Entry is free. Allow about an hour.
  3. Afternoon option: Anjengo Fort. Go south to Anchuthengu, or Anjengo, an early East India Company trade settlement. Recent itineraries list entry or parking at about Rs 20 to Rs 50. It is a quiet, short stop, not a half-day attraction.
  4. Evening: Last cliff dinner or departure. Varkala Sivagiri station is close to town, so an evening train works well and keeps the trip to 1 night.

Where to stay: Varkala stay tiers

North Cliff is the convenient base: beach steps, food and shops on foot. South Cliff is quieter. Hostel listings in 2026 start from under US$4 for dorms on Hostelworld, matching the budget band below. Book a cliff-view room only if you will spend time in it.

How to reach Varkala and what it costs

The nearest airport is Thiruvananthapuram International Airport, about 42 to 45 km away by road. Most visitors come via Trivandrum city, which keeps the options simple: train for value, taxi for door-to-door ease.

Best time for this 2-day plan

October to March is the easiest window, about 20 to 30 degrees in our sources. In monsoon, June to September, expect rough seas, some shacks closed, and lower rates: swimming and boating may stop. In April and May, start beach time early.

Tips that save time and money

  1. Do not pay for a full-day cab. Varkala rewards walking: keep Day 1 on foot and hire an auto only for the Day 2 Kappil and Anjengo loop.
  2. Skip a paid sunset point. The North Cliff sunset is free and is the view people come for.
  3. Share boat costs correctly. Kappil-area boats are often priced per boat, so a group of four pays much less per person than a couple hiring alone.
  4. Eat one meal a day away from the cliff strip. Town eateries near the temple and station are cheaper than cliff cafes for the same Kerala meals.
  5. Check the sea before you swim, every time. A free beach day is not a saving if conditions are rough.

FAQ

How much does a Varkala trip cost for 2 days?

About Rs 1,800 to Rs 3,300 per person budget and Rs 2,850 to Rs 5,000 mid-range for 2 days and 1 night, excluding travel to Varkala. A cliff-view room pushes it to Rs 5,000 to Rs 8,200 per person.

Is 2 days enough for Varkala?

Yes, for the cliff, beach, temple, Sivagiri and one outing to Kappil or Anjengo. Add a third day for a full Ayurveda course or surf lessons.

Where should I stay for a 2-day Varkala trip?

On or just behind the North Cliff for a first visit, so you can walk to food, beach steps and sunset. Choose South Cliff or the temple side only if quiet matters more than choice of cafes.

What is free to do in Varkala?

The cliff walk and sunset, Papanasam Beach, Janardhana Swamy Temple and Sivagiri Mutt are free. You mainly pay for stay, food, local autos and any boat, kayak or massage you choose.

How do I reach Varkala from Trivandrum airport?

The airport is about 42 to 45 km away. Take a taxi direct, about Rs 950 to Rs 1,200 from Trivandrum city in listings, or go into the city and take the hourly train at Rs 120 to Rs 950 by class.

Can I do Varkala as a day trip from Trivandrum?

You can, the train takes about 33 to 40 minutes, but you would miss cliff sunset and a slow beach morning. One night is the minimum that feels complete.

Honest caveat: headline sights are free, but a cliff-view room in December or January and one resort-style dinner can double a 2-day total. Price your room tier first.
