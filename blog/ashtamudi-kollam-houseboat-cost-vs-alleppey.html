Ashtamudi and Kollam Houseboat and Backwater Day Trips: Cost vs Alleppey

Ashtamudi Lake is Kerala's quieter backwater: a shared day cruise from Rs 400, a day houseboat with meals from Rs 1,550, and no need to pay Alleppey overnight prices unless sleeping on the water is the point.

For a backwater day out, Kollam is cheaper and quieter than Alleppey. A shared Ashtamudi day cruise costs Rs 400 to Rs 500 per person on the state water transport boat, or from Rs 1,550 per person on a private-style day houseboat with meals. A private overnight houseboat is Alleppey's product: Kollam has far fewer overnight boats, and no current published Kollam overnight tariff could be verified, so this guide does not invent one. Want a bedroom night on the water? Book Alleppey. Want a calm lake, a long day cruise, and a lower bill? Go to Kollam.

What Ashtamudi Lake offers that Alleppey does not

Ashtamudi Lake, in Kollam district, is Kerala's second-largest lake. Its name refers to eight braids, or channels, from the main lake, and it is a Ramsar-listed wetland. Boats move between open lake water, narrow canals, and island stops such as Munroe Island and Sambranikodi, instead of one busy canal corridor. Alleppey has more boats and choice; Kollam has fewer boats, wider views, and a slower pace.

Kollam also suits a south Kerala route: it sits between Alleppey and Trivandrum, so it pairs with Varkala or Trivandrum, while Alleppey pairs with Kochi and Kumarakom. Pick one water experience per day.

Private overnight houseboats: Kollam vs Alleppey

This is where the comparison turns in Alleppey's favour. Alleppey has hundreds of private houseboats in Deluxe, Premium, and Luxury classes, with check-in at noon and check-out at 9:00 AM, all meals included, and a 5 to 6 hour cruise on day one plus a short morning cruise. Kerala tariff lists commonly quote a 1-bedroom Deluxe overnight from about Rs 8,500 and a 2-bedroom Deluxe from about Rs 12,500, before GST. Our Alleppey group guide puts a 3-bedroom boat for 6 at Rs 18,000 to Rs 21,000 a night in Deluxe, Rs 26,000 to Rs 27,000 in Premium, and Rs 36,000 to Rs 42,000 in Luxury, all meals included.

The practical rule is simple. If sleeping on a houseboat is the point, pay Alleppey prices and book early. If the point is a backwater day and lunch on the water, Kollam gives you that for a fraction of the overnight price, and you sleep in a Kollam hotel that night.

The Kollam to Alleppey full-day cruise

The Kollam to Alappuzha cruise is the longest classic backwater journey in Kerala, at about 8 hours on the water, passing villages, paddy edges, and coir-making hamlets. DTPC Kollam lists it as a one-way trip with a full day and night stay, and also lists a 2-day, 2-night Kollam to Alleppey to Kochi option. You board in Kollam and finish in Alleppey, so you need a hotel in Alleppey that night. DTPC also lists two Kollam overnight formats: See and Sleep, 2:00 PM to 8:00 AM, and Star Night, 5:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Their current tariff was not published on the page read for this post, so get price, meals, and AC hours in writing before paying.

How to reach Kollam for a day trip

Kollam is easier from Trivandrum than from Kochi. From Trivandrum it is about 70 km, about 1 hour by train and about 2 hours by road. From Kochi it is about 142 to 146 km, and the train is the sensible choice.

For a day cruise, arrive the night before or take an early train: a 10:00 AM or 11:00 AM boarding leaves no room for a late Kochi departure. Kollam Junction is the railhead; take a taxi or auto to the boat jetty area on Link Road.

How to book, and what to check before paying

  1. Decide first: shared day boat, private-style day houseboat, or overnight format. Do not compare a Rs 400 shared ticket with an Rs 8,500 overnight as if they were the same product.
  2. For the See Ashtamudi shared boat, confirm sailing day and deck price in advance; Kerala Tourism lists advance seat reservation.
  3. For a day houseboat, confirm boarding time, cruise route, meal list, island stop charges, and whether the price is per person or per boat.
  4. For any DTPC overnight format, get tariff, meals, AC hours, and timings in writing. Older DTPC prices online are not used here.
  5. For a one-way Kollam to Alleppey cruise, confirm disembarkation point, luggage handling, and where you sleep that night.

Best time to visit is October to April, when the lake is calmest and the heat is easier than in summer. Carry water and sunscreen, and keep phones in a dry bag on small country craft.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kollam cheaper than Alleppey for a houseboat?

For a day trip, yes: shared Rs 400 to Rs 500, private-style day houseboat from Rs 1,550 with meals, both well below a private overnight. For a bedroom night, Alleppey has published tariffs from about Rs 8,500 for a 1-bedroom Deluxe.

Can I stay overnight on a Kollam houseboat?

DTPC lists See and Sleep and Star Night formats, but no current tariff was on the page read, so confirm price before booking. The main private day operator does not offer overnight stays.

How long is the Kollam to Alleppey cruise?

About 8 hours, one way, with a full day and night stay in the DTPC format. Plan a hotel in Alleppey that night.

Is Ashtamudi better than Alleppey for families?

For a calm, lower-cost day, Ashtamudi is often easier, with wide lake views and island stops. To sleep on the boat, Alleppey has far more bedroom boats.

Do I need to book a Kollam day cruise in advance?

Yes in peak season and on weekends, especially for a day houseboat. Shared boats can also fill, so reserve where offered.
