Poovar Island Day Trip from Trivandrum Cost

A Poovar Island day trip from Trivandrum costs about Rs 2,500 per private boat for 1 hour of backwater boating, or Rs 3,500 for 1.5 hours, plus the 32 km drive from the city: about Rs 2,300 one way by sedan taxi at listed online rates. There is no separate entry fee for the island or the beach. For two people sharing one boat and a return taxi, budget roughly Rs 6,000 to Rs 8,100 before food.

Last updated: October 2026

The short answer: what the day trip costs

Poovar is a small coastal village about 30 to 32 km south of Trivandrum city, where the Neyyar River meets the Arabian Sea. The village and its golden sand beach sit across the backwaters, so the boat is not an optional extra: it is how you see the place. Prices below are per private boat, not per person, from published boating listings and recent visitor reports checked for this guide in October 2026.

Worked total for two people: a 1.5 hour boat at Rs 3,500 plus two one-way sedan trips at about Rs 2,300 each comes to about Rs 8,100 before food. With a 1 hour boat and a return arrangement negotiated with the same driver, the same trip lands closer to Rs 6,000 to Rs 7,000. Splitting one boat between four or five people cuts the per-person boating cost sharply, because the fare is for the boat. Add meals on top: floating restaurants and village eateries price dishes individually, so confirm prices before you order.

How to reach Poovar from Trivandrum

1. Taxi or app cab: the simplest option. The drive is about 32 km via the Kovalam bypass side of the city and takes roughly 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on traffic. Ask the driver to wait at the jetty or fix the return pickup before you board the boat.

2. KSRTC bus: buses run from East Fort in Trivandrum toward Poovar and Neyyattinkara, and an auto from the Poovar stop covers the last stretch to the jetty. This is by far the cheapest way to go, but allow extra time each way and check the return bus timing at the jetty before your boat ride.

3. Combine it with Kovalam: Poovar is only about 12 km south of Kovalam, so a Kovalam stay makes this an easy half-day outing rather than a full day trip. Our Kovalam vs Varkala comparison covers the two beach bases nearest to Poovar.

There is no railway station at Poovar. The nearest practical railhead is Trivandrum Central, from where you continue by road.

Boat charges: how the pricing actually works

Boating at Poovar runs through the day, with published timings of about 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. You hire the whole boat at the jetty: country boats, motorboats, and larger cruise boats all operate, and the motorboat is the standard choice for a day trip. The listed rates are Rs 2,500 for 1 hour and Rs 3,500 for 1.5 hours, and recent visitors report paying Rs 2,000 to Rs 3,000 for a 1 hour ride, so the first quote is not always the final price.

Three habits keep the cost honest. First, agree the duration, the route, and the total price before you step in, and confirm whether the Golden Sand Beach stop is included. Second, be wary of touts near the jetty steering you to a specific operator or upselling a much longer ride: one recent visitor report describes being pushed into a 3 hour sunset package at Rs 6,500. A 1 to 1.5 hour ride covers the full standard route. Third, pay the boat operator directly rather than through a middleman where you can.

What the boat fare includes

1. A private boat with a boatman for the agreed duration, cruising the Neyyar River backwaters from the Poovar jetty.

2. The mangrove channels: narrow, green stretches that are the quietest and most scenic part of the ride, with kingfishers and other water birds a common sight.

3. Views of the floating cottages and floating restaurants on the backwaters, and of Elephant Rock across the water.

4. A stop at Golden Sand Beach, the sandbar where the river, the backwaters, and the Arabian Sea meet. This beach is reachable only by boat.

Not included: food and drinks (floating restaurants bill separately), entry to any resort property, and your road transfer from Trivandrum. Swimming at the estuary beach is not part of the trip: currents where the river meets the sea are strong, so treat the beach as a walk-and-photos stop and follow the boatman's guidance on where to stand.

A realistic half-day plan

Leave Trivandrum by 8:00 to 8:30 am and you reach the jetty before the midday heat. Take the 1.5 hour boat: out through the mangroves, a stop at Golden Sand Beach, and back past the floating cottages. Lunch at a floating restaurant or back in the village, and you are back in the city by mid-afternoon. That leaves the evening free, which is why Poovar pairs well with the city sights in our Trivandrum 2 day itinerary: do the temple and museums on one day and Poovar on the other. The alternative slot is late afternoon, when the light over the estuary is at its best, but boats wind down by about 5:30 pm, so do not leave the city after 3 pm.

Best time to go

October to March brings the most comfortable weather for a boat ride and a beach stop. Mornings suit birdwatching in the mangroves; late afternoons suit the estuary light. In the monsoon months the sea end of the trip is rougher, beach stops can be shortened, and rides depend on the day's conditions, so keep the plan flexible. Poovar is also a resort base in its own right, known for backwater stays and Ayurveda: if a treatment day appeals, our Kerala Ayurveda treatment cost guide prices single sessions and full programmes.

FAQ

How much does a Poovar Island day trip from Trivandrum cost for 2 people?

About Rs 6,000 to Rs 8,100 before food: Rs 2,500 to Rs 3,500 for a private boat for 1 to 1.5 hours, plus about Rs 2,300 each way by sedan taxi at listed online rates. Going by KSRTC bus instead of taxi cuts the transport share to a small fraction of that.

Is the Poovar boat price per person or per boat?

Per boat. The standard listed rates of Rs 2,500 for 1 hour and Rs 3,500 for 1.5 hours hire the whole private motorboat, so a group of four pays the same as a couple and splits it four ways.

How long do you need at Poovar?

Half a day is enough: about an hour of driving each way from Trivandrum, a 1 to 1.5 hour boat ride, and time for lunch. A full day only makes sense if you add Kovalam, about 12 km away, on the same outing.

Can you swim at Poovar beach?

No, plan not to. Golden Sand Beach sits at the estuary where the Neyyar River meets the Arabian Sea, and the currents there are strong. Enjoy it as a scenic stop, and swim instead at a lifeguarded beach such as Kovalam.

Do you need to book the Poovar boat in advance?

Usually no. Boats are hired at the Poovar jetty on arrival, and published timings run from about 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. In peak season, arriving before 10 am avoids the wait and the midday heat.

Is Poovar worth it if you are also visiting Alleppey?

Yes, as a different kind of ride: Poovar is a short mangrove and estuary cruise ending at a sea-facing sandbar, while Alleppey is a long canal and lake cruise on a houseboat. If you only have time for one backwater experience on a Trivandrum-based trip, Poovar is the one that fits a half day.
