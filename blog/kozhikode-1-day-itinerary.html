Kozhikode (Calicut) 1 Day Itinerary

One day in Kozhikode is enough for the city core plus one beach, if you keep the order tight: Tali Temple and Mananchira Square in the morning, a Malabar biryani lunch and SM Street in the middle of the day, then Kappad Beach, about 16 km north, for the late afternoon. The paid sights are cheap: museums list entry at Rs 20 to Rs 40 per person and Kozhikkodan halwa is Rs 80 to Rs 180 per kg, so the day is decided by transport, with a full-day sedan sightseeing cab listed from Rs 2,999 for 8 to 9 hours.

Last updated: October 2026

Kozhikode, still widely called Calicut, is the old Malabar Coast spice port, and its sights split into two clusters. The first is walkable in the centre: Mananchira Square, Tali Temple, SM Street and the East Hill museums. The second is the coast, where one-day plans go wrong: Kappad to the north and Beypore to the south are in opposite directions, so pick one. Kappad for history, Beypore for the wooden uru shipyards.

The beaches, Mananchira, Tali Temple and SM Street list no entry fee, so a traveller who walks the centre and buses to Kappad can do the day for well under the cab column. What the cab buys is the Kappad leg and the museums without waiting for buses in the heat.

The 1 day itinerary, hour by hour

  1. Morning, Tali Temple and Mananchira Square: Start with Tali Shiva Temple, the Zamorin-era temple in the city centre, then walk to Mananchira Square, the park around the old Mananchira tank next door. Temple dress rules apply, so read our Kerala temple dress code guide before you go, and note that the temple is a place of worship first, not a photo stop.
  2. Late morning, East Hill museums or Sarovaram: Choose one. The Pazhassi Raja Museum (listed at Rs 20) and the Krishna Menon Museum (listed at Rs 40) sit together at East Hill and suit history travellers. Sarovaram Bio Park (listed at Rs 30), a wetland park with walking trails and boating, suits families and anyone who wants a slower hour. Both museum listings show Monday as the weekly holiday, so on a Monday do Sarovaram instead and do not build the day around East Hill.
  3. Midday, Malabar lunch: Order the local biryani, seafood in coconut-based gravies, or a banana-leaf meal, and keep it unhurried: midday is hot on the coast, and lunch is the sensible indoor break.
  4. Afternoon, SM Street: SM Street, short for Sweetmeat Street and also called Mithai Theravu, is the shopping street by Mananchira, with many shops listing hours around 9 am to 10 pm. This is where Kozhikkodan halwa is made and sold: Kerala Tourism describes it as a coconut-oil halwa, unlike ghee-based halwas elsewhere in India, priced typically at Rs 80 to Rs 180 per kg. Taste a sample, buy the classic black or a fruit flavour, and add banana chips and spices if you are shopping for home.
  5. Late afternoon, Kappad Beach: Drive about 16 km north to Kappad. Kerala Tourism records that a plaque here marks where Vasco da Gama landed on 27 May 1498, with a rocky headland and an old temple on the rock as the other sights. It is a quiet beach, best for a walk in the late light. Allow an hour, then drive back for dinner or an evening train.

The Beypore swap: If shipbuilding interests you more than the 1498 landing, replace Kappad with Beypore, about 10 km south of the centre, where wooden uru vessels are still built. Keep it a straight swap: doing both in one afternoon means spending it crossing the city.

How the Kozhikode day fits a Wayanad trip

Kozhikode is the usual gateway to Wayanad: it is the nearest major railhead, and the road onward runs via NH766 through the Thamarassery Churam, the ghat section with 9 hairpin bends. Kalpetta, the first Wayanad town most travellers use, is about 85 km away, listed at about 2 hours 40 minutes to 3.5 hours depending on traffic, weather and your exact destination in Wayanad. One-way taxi listings for Kozhikode to Wayanad typically sit around Rs 2,500 to Rs 4,500, with some booking sites listing fares starting lower, from about Rs 2,170, depending on car type and destination. KSRTC and private buses run the same route for much less.

Do not add Wayanad sightseeing to the end of this day: you would reach the ghats tired and in the dark. The pattern that works is this city day, a night in Kozhikode, an early start up the churam next morning, then the plan in our Wayanad 2 day itinerary.

Best time for a Kozhikode day trip

October to March is the comfortable window for a walking-heavy city day, matching the statewide picture in our best time to visit Kerala, month by month. In monsoon the centre is still doable, as the museums, lunch and SM Street are under cover, but cut the beaches first if rain is heavy. In summer, start earlier and leave Kappad to late afternoon, as this itinerary already does.

FAQ

Is 1 day enough for Kozhikode?

Yes, for the city-centre sights, SM Street food and shopping, and one beach excursion to Kappad or Beypore. It is not enough for both beach directions plus the museums without rushing, and it does not include Wayanad, which needs its own days after the 85 km ghat drive.

What is Kappad Beach famous for, and how far is it?

Kappad, about 16 km from Kozhikode town, is where a plaque marks Vasco da Gama's landing on 27 May 1498, per Kerala Tourism. Entry is free, and most visitors spend about an hour walking the beach and the rocky headland.

How much does Kozhikkodan halwa cost on SM Street?

Kerala Tourism puts Kozhikkodan halwa typically at Rs 80 to Rs 180 per kg, depending on flavour and ingredients. It is made with coconut oil, keeps without refrigeration, and is the one food souvenir to buy here rather than anywhere else in Kerala.

What should I eat in Kozhikode in one day?

Make lunch the main meal: Malabar biryani or a seafood meal in the centre, beach and street snacks if you pass them, and halwa from SM Street to take home. Budget Rs 500 to Rs 750 per person for the day eating locally, or Rs 800 to Rs 1,200 at mid-range restaurants, per our food guide ranges.

Can I do Kozhikode and Wayanad in the same day?

Not sensibly. Kozhikode to Kalpetta alone is about 85 km and roughly 2.5 to 3.5 hours each way through the Thamarassery Churam ghat. Spend the day in Kozhikode, sleep there, and start for Wayanad early the next morning.

Are the Kozhikode museums open on Monday?

The listings for the Pazhassi Raja Museum and the Krishna Menon Museum at East Hill both show Monday as closed. On a Monday, swap that slot for Sarovaram Bio Park, listed as open daily from 9 am, and keep the rest of the day unchanged.
