Kerala Honeymoon Cost for 7 Days, Self-Planned

A self-planned 7-day Kerala honeymoon for two typically costs Rs.55,000 to Rs.65,000 on a budget, Rs.90,000 to Rs.1,20,000 at mid-range, and Rs.1,60,000 or more at premium. Those totals exclude flights or trains to Kerala. Here is where every rupee goes.

The straight answer: what a DIY Kerala honeymoon costs

These are per-couple totals for 6 nights and 7 days, booked yourself, excluding travel to and from Kerala. They assume the classic route: Kochi to Munnar (2 nights), Thekkady (1 night), an Alleppey houseboat (1 night), and a beach finish at Marari or Kovalam (2 nights).

For reference, current 7-day Kerala honeymoon packages start from about Rs.27,500 per person, or roughly Rs.55,000 per couple, with premium resorts and private houseboats pushing the total much higher. Those packages generally exclude flights, entry tickets, and optional activities, so a package quote is not directly comparable to a fully self-planned trip.

A sample 7-day honeymoon route

Day 1: Land in Kochi, easy evening at Fort Kochi. Day 2: Drive to Munnar (about 4 hours) through the Cheeyappara waterfalls. Day 3: Eravikulam National Park and the Tata Tea Museum. Day 4: Drive to Thekkady; Periyar boating and a spice plantation walk. Day 5: Drive to Alleppey and board your houseboat by afternoon. Day 6: Disembark, drive to Marari or Kovalam for the beach finish. Day 7: Fly or train out. This is essentially the classic 7-day circuit; our 5-day Munnar to Alleppey itinerary covers the hills-to-backwaters core if you want to stretch it to seven.

Honest caveat: seven days is enough for the classic circuit, but it is not enough for the circuit plus Wayanad or a deep Kochi stop. Couples who try to add a fourth destination end up spending the honeymoon in a car. Pick hills, backwaters, and one beach; that is the trip.

Honeymoon extras: worth it or not

Candlelight dinner on the houseboat: worth it once. Most premium houseboats will arrange one; confirm the price in writing before boarding, not after. Ayurveda couple massage: Rs.3,000+ per session, and genuinely one of Kerala's signature experiences. Honeymoon room decor (flowers and cake): most mid-range hotels do this free if you mention it is your honeymoon when booking. Sunset Kathakali performance in Kochi at Rs.300 to Rs.500 per person is cheap and memorable, while the overpriced "private shikara tours" sold on the Alleppey jetty are skippable: a shared sunset shikara does the same thing for a fraction of the price.

When you go changes the price

December and January are the most expensive months: hotels and houseboats price at the top of their ranges, while shoulder-season bookings land near the bottom. October to November and February to March are the best value window, with pleasant weather and saner prices. June to September (monsoon) is the cheapest and the most romantic in its own way, with misty hills and full waterfalls, but houseboat cruising is restricted on heavy-rain days and Munnar viewpoints often sit inside clouds.

Self-planned vs a package

Packages win on convenience: one payment covers hotels, cab, and houseboat, and the operator handles the routing. Self-planning wins on control: you pick the exact hotel, the exact houseboat, and skip the commission-built "complimentary" sightseeing stops that packages sneak in. For honeymoons specifically, self-planning also lets you splurge exactly where it matters to you (the houseboat night, one resort) and save everywhere else. Either way, get the houseboat's category and AC hours in writing before you pay: Kerala Tourism classifies boats as Gold, Silver, and Green Palm, and "AC houseboat" sometimes means AC only from 9 PM to 6 AM on Silver-category boats.

FAQ

Is Rs.1,00,000 enough for a Kerala honeymoon?

Yes, for a self-planned 7-day trip at mid-range excluding flights. It covers 3 to 4-star hotels, a private sedan, a premium houseboat night, and mid-range dining, with a small buffer.

What is the best month for a honeymoon in Kerala?

October to March is the classic window: cool, dry, and reliable. October to November and February to March give you the best balance of weather and price; December and January cost the most.

Is 7 days enough for a Kerala honeymoon?

Yes for the classic Kochi to Munnar to Thekkady to Alleppey to beach circuit. It is not enough to add Wayanad or a second beach town without turning the trip into a driving marathon.

Houseboat or resort for the honeymoon night?

The houseboat night is the one Kerala experience couples remember, and it is priced for two rather than per person on most boats, which makes it reasonable value. Book premium or above for privacy and a good onboard kitchen.

Is Kerala safe for honeymoon couples?

Yes. Kerala is consistently rated one of India's safest states for travellers, with good roads, reliable tourist infrastructure, and a strong hospitality culture.

Is Kerala worth visiting in the monsoon for a honeymoon?

It can be: lower prices, lush scenery, and Ayurveda season. The trade-off is rain-disrupted plans, restricted houseboat cruising on heavy-rain days, and cloudy hill viewpoints.
