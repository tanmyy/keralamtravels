Munnar to Alleppey Taxi Fare One Way

A one-way taxi from Munnar to Alleppey costs roughly Rs 1,682 to Rs 5,849 for a hatchback or sedan, and Rs 2,430 to Rs 7,991 for an SUV or Innova, for the 162 to 170 km drive that takes 4 to 6 hours. The wide range comes down to one thing: full-service operators bill you for the car's return trip too, while one-way-drop aggregators charge only for the kilometres you ride.

What a one-way cab from Munnar to Alleppey costs

Two pricing models dominate this route, and their quotes look nothing alike. Aggregators sell cheap one-way drops where you pay only for the distance covered. Traditional operators publish a full fare breakup where the empty return leg is billed at nearly the full per-km rate. Both are real prices for the same 162 to 170 km road.

The Rs 300 driver allowance and 5% GST sit on top. Tolls and parking are extra.

Why one-way costs roughly double per km

The car still has to get home. Most outstation taxis in Kerala are charged from garage to garage, so a one-way drop leaves the driver with an empty 170 km return to Munnar. Operators handle that in two ways: bill the return leg at nearly the full per-km rate (the breakup above), or absorb it into a flat drop fare that still quietly prices in the dead kilometres. Either way, you pay for more than the 162 km you ride.

The other built-in line item is the driver bata, the daily allowance. Published one-way breakups on this route show Rs 300 for the day, separate from fuel and per-km charges. Then there is GST at 5% on the fare total for operators who itemise it.

Two rules of the road you should know before booking

AC is switched off in the hills. Operators state this openly in their terms: on the Munnar ghat sections, the air-conditioning stays off so the engine can handle the climbs. From Adimali down to the plains it comes back on. Do not treat this as a complaint point; treat it as a confirmed term and pack accordingly.

Tolls are one-sided on one-way drops. Aggregators confirm you pay only one side of toll tax on a one-way cab, not the return leg. On the full-breakup model, tolls and parking are not included in the quoted total at all, so budget a few hundred rupees extra for them.

Honest caveat: the cheapest drop fares are dynamic and subject to availability, and they assume a fixed kilometre limit. Extra kilometres beyond it are billed per km on both sides in the breakup model (for example, Rs 30 per km for a hatchback, Rs 43 for an SUV). Confirm your kilometre cap in writing before you pay the advance, which is usually 10 to 20% of the fare.

How to book without surprises

  1. Ask which model you are buying. A drop fare and a fare-breakup quote for the same route differ by Rs 3,000 or more. Make sure you are comparing like with like.
  2. Confirm the kilometre cap. One-way fares carry a fixed kilometre limit (around 162 to 170 km here). Know the per-km rate beyond it.
  3. Pin down the extras. Tolls (one side or excluded?), parking, and night charges, and whether the driver allowance is inside the quote or added on top.
  4. Book a day ahead. Cabs can be booked up to an hour before departure, but booking one day in advance avoids the last-minute rush, and driver and car details usually arrive within minutes of booking.

Frequently asked questions

What is the distance from Munnar to Alleppey by road?

Operators quote 162 to 170 km depending on the route taken, via NH85 and the Panvel-Kochi-Kanyakumari highway. The drive takes 4 to 6 hours depending on traffic.

Is the one-way drop fare really cheaper than a round trip?

Yes, but not by half. A drop fare saves you the return-leg billing, which is why aggregator drops start near Rs 1,682 while full-breakup one-way quotes reach Rs 5,849 for a sedan. If you are returning to Munnar the same trip, compare the drop against a round-trip quote before deciding.

Does the one-way cab include sightseeing stops?

No. A pure one-way drop covers only Munnar and Alleppey; sightseeing is excluded. If you want waterfall stops at Cheeyappara and Valara or the spice gardens at Adimali on the way, book a one-way multi-stop cab, which adds extra charges for the additional stops.

Do I pay toll tax on both sides for a one-way cab?

No. On the one-way-drop model you pay toll for one side only. On the fare-breakup model, tolls are excluded from the quoted total and paid as applicable.

Why is the AC off in Munnar?

It is a standard operator term, not a malfunction: the AC is switched off on hill sections so the engine is not strained on the climbs. It runs normally on the plains leg of the drive.
