Kerala Trip Cost for 3 Days

For 2 people sharing one room, a self-planned 3-day Kerala trip costs about Rs 23,000 to Rs 33,000 on a budget, Rs 34,000 to Rs 50,000 at mid-range, and Rs 65,000 or more for luxury, excluding travel to Kerala. Stay in one base and skip the overnight houseboat and the same 3 days can cost Rs 10,000 to Rs 18,000 for two. The route you pick matters more than the hotel tier. Last updated: October 2026.

Last updated: October 2026. All prices are planning estimates from 2026 operator listings and published cost guides, not quotes. Confirm hotel, cab and boat rates before you pay an advance.

What does a 3-day Kerala trip cost for 2 people?

Three days means 2 nights. Most first-timers fit Kochi, Munnar and Alleppey into those nights: one night in Munnar and one on a houseboat, with Kochi sightseeing around arrival and departure. The loop works, but driving drives the cost: you pay for a cab for all 3 days even though you sleep in only two places.

For a cheaper, calmer trip, pick one base instead: Kochi, Kumarakom or Munnar alone. The tables below price both approaches.

Cheaper option: stay in one place for 3 days

A single-base trip removes most of the cab bill and both hotel changes. A published 2026 Kumarakom budget for 2 people over 3 days and 2 nights totals Rs 9,500 to Rs 12,400 at budget level and Rs 16,300 to Rs 26,500 at a more comfortable level, excluding travel to Kumarakom. That breakdown uses a basic AC room at Rs 2,800 per night, food at about Rs 2,400 to Rs 4,500 for the stay, and a 2-hour shikara cruise at Rs 1,400 for two instead of an overnight houseboat.

A Munnar-only 3 days sits between the two: recent 2026 Munnar cost research puts 3 days for two at Rs 12,310 budget and Rs 27,320 mid-range, including hotel, food, local transport and sightseeing but excluding travel to Munnar. Entry fees there are small: Eravikulam National Park is listed at Rs 200 per person in that research. See our Munnar 2 day itinerary budget for a day-by-day version you can extend by one night.

A realistic 3-day plan that fits the budget

  1. Day 1: Arrive in Kochi and drive to Munnar. Start early, stop at the waterfalls on the way, check in by afternoon and keep sightseeing light. Your cab and hotel night are the main costs today.
  2. Day 2: Munnar in the morning, Alleppey by noon. Fit in the tea gardens and one paid sight in Munnar, then drive to Alleppey to board the houseboat around midday. Lunch, dinner and the overnight stay are included in the houseboat fare.
  3. Day 3: Houseboat checkout and Fort Kochi. Boats check out in the morning. Use the remaining hours for Fort Kochi and Mattancherry, then head to the airport or station. Keep a buffer: do not book a tight departure.

If that pace sounds rushed, it is. Our guide to how many days are enough for Kerala recommends 5 days as the comfortable minimum for this same circuit. In 3 days, dropping Thekkady and Kovalam is not a compromise, it is what makes the trip work.

What is not included in these totals

Flights or trains to Kochi, insurance, shopping and houseboat extras such as special seafood are extra. Airport transfers are also extra if your cab package does not start at the airport: published guides put a Kochi airport taxi at about Rs 2,800 to Rs 3,500 one way. How season changes a 3-day price

December and January are the most expensive weeks, especially for houseboats and hill hotels. October to November and February to March offer better value. Monsoon, June to September, brings the lowest rates, but rain can close viewpoints and slow hill roads, which hurts a tight 3-day plan most. In monsoon, the single-base plan is the safer spend.

How to keep a 3-day trip on budget

  1. Choose one route and price it fully: loop with houseboat, or single base without. Mixing both is where totals jump.
  2. Book the cab as one 3-day package rather than separate one-way drops. One-way legs are billed for the return journey and cost more per kilometre.
  3. Keep the houseboat at deluxe level unless full-time AC or decor matters to you. The route and meals are similar across tiers; the upgrade mainly buys finish and AC hours.
  4. Eat the big meal at local restaurants at lunch and avoid resort dining for every meal. One resort dinner for two can cost as much as a full local food day.
  5. Run your own dates and hotel tier through the trip budget planner and compare it with any package quote line by line.

FAQ

How much does a Kerala trip cost for 3 days for 2 people?

About Rs 23,000 to Rs 33,000 budget, Rs 34,000 to Rs 50,000 mid-range and Rs 65,000 or more luxury for the Kochi, Munnar and Alleppey loop, excluding travel to Kerala. A single-base 3 days without an overnight houseboat can cost Rs 9,500 to Rs 26,500 for two, depending on comfort level.

Is 3 days enough for Kerala?

Enough for one circuit done briskly or one place done well, not enough for hills, backwaters and the coast together. Pick either the Munnar plus houseboat loop or a single base in Kochi, Kumarakom or Munnar.

What is the cheapest 3-day Kerala plan?

Stay in one place, use local buses or short auto rides, eat locally, and swap the overnight houseboat for a 2-hour shikara cruise. The Kumarakom example above totals Rs 9,500 to Rs 12,400 for two before travel to Kumarakom.

Does the houseboat price include food?

Yes, on an overnight boat lunch, dinner and next-morning breakfast are included in the standard fare. Special seafood and other extras may be charged separately, so confirm inclusions in writing when you book.

How much should I keep per day for food?

About Rs 500 to Rs 750 per person per day eating at local restaurants, and Rs 800 to Rs 1,200 per person per day at mid-range restaurants. Houseboat days are already covered in the boat fare.

Honest caveat: a 3-day total is unusually sensitive to one choice: the houseboat night and the 3-day cab together are more than half the loop budget. Get both quotes in writing for your exact dates before comparing any package headline price, and confirm what meals, AC hours and transfers are included.
