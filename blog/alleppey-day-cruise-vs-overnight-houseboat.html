Alleppey Day Cruise vs Overnight Houseboat: Price Difference

A 6-hour day cruise on a private 1-bedroom boat costs about Rs 7,000 to 10,000 per couple for a deluxe boat in peak season; the 21-hour overnight stay on the same boat costs about Rs 10,000 to 13,000. The overnight adds roughly Rs 2,000 to 5,000 at every tier, and buys you dinner, breakfast, the sunset, and a morning on the water. Last updated: September 2026.

Price difference at a glance

All figures below are for a private 1-bedroom boat for 2 people, the standard unit operators quote. Prices are peak-season ranges (October to March) from operator tariff pages we read in September 2026. Off-season (April to September) usually runs 20 to 40 percent lower.

Boat tierDay cruise (5-6 hrs)Overnight (21 hrs)What the extra money buys
DeluxeRs 7,000 - 10,000Rs 10,000 - 13,000Dinner, breakfast, sunset and sunrise on the water
PremiumRs 9,000 - 12,000Rs 12,000 - 16,000Full-time AC, multi-dish menu, more deck space
LuxuryRs 12,000 - 18,000Rs 18,000 - 25,000+Boutique interiors, gourmet menu, upper lounge

The pattern holds across tiers: the overnight stay costs roughly 30 to 40 percent more than the day cruise on the same boat. One source's overnight tariff (Beautiful Alleppey, 2026) lists Rs 10,000 for a 1-bedroom deluxe and Rs 14,000 for premium per couple, which sits inside the ranges above. Expect about 5 percent GST on top, charged separately by most operators. Peak-holiday dates (Onam, Diwali, Christmas to New Year) can push both options up by 50 percent to double, so confirm the rate in writing for December dates.

What a day cruise includes (and misses)

A day cruise typically runs about 11 am to 5 pm, roughly 5 to 6 hours on the water. The package usually covers a welcome drink, a Keralan lunch, and evening tea with snacks. You see the paddy fields, narrow canals, and village life, and you are back on land the same evening.

What you do not get: dinner, breakfast, the sunset over the backwaters, and the stillness of the night. Boats anchor by evening, so the day cruise ends before dusk. Air conditioning on deluxe day boats is usually not a full-day affair, which matters on warm afternoons. If your itinerary is packed (Munnar to Alleppey in one day, say), the day cruise is the only realistic option, and that is the main case for it.

What the overnight stay adds

The overnight is a noon-to-9-am affair, about 21 hours. You check in around 12 noon, cruise through the afternoon, anchor by evening, and cruise again the next morning before breakfast and checkout at 9 am. The package covers welcome drink, lunch, evening tea and snacks, dinner, and breakfast the next morning: four meals plus snacks instead of the day cruise's two.

The real difference is not the meals, it is the hours. You get the sunset from the deck, the quiet night anchored near the bank, and a short morning cruise when the water is calmest. On a deluxe boat, AC usually runs 9 pm to 6 am in the bedroom; premium and luxury boats generally run AC full time. The budget alternative: sharing cruises

If a private boat feels expensive, sharing boats run the same routes with a private bedroom for your group and shared deck and dining. Reported rates (January 2026) run Rs 700 to 1,500 per person for a 4 to 5 hour day cruise, and Rs 2,000 to 4,500 per person for an overnight with all meals included. The price difference between the two is even smaller per person here, which makes the overnight the easy choice if you can spare the night.

Which one should you pick?

  • Pick the day cruise if you are short on days, arriving late in Alleppey, or want to keep the backwaters to a single afternoon before moving on. It delivers the core scenery at the lowest price.
  • Pick the overnight if this is your first backwater trip, it is a honeymoon, or you have a full day free. The price jump is modest next to the extra meals and hours, and the night on the water is the part people remember.
  • Skip both for a shikara if you just want 2 to 3 hours among the narrow canals: small covered boats charge roughly Rs 500 to 600 per hour and reach canals houseboats cannot enter.
Honest caveat: operator websites quote peak-season ranges, and the final rate always depends on your date and availability. Short cruises of 2 to 4 hours exist too (roughly Rs 4,000 to 12,000 depending on duration), mostly bookable in the off-season. Get the rate, the AC timings, and the meal list confirmed in writing before paying any advance.

FAQs

Is the overnight houseboat worth the extra money?
For most first-time visitors, yes. The day cruise and overnight differ by about Rs 2,000 to 5,000 on a deluxe boat, and the overnight adds dinner, breakfast, sunset, and a morning cruise.

What time does a day cruise start and end?
Typically around 11 am to 5 pm, about 5 to 6 hours. Overnight boats check in at 12 noon and check out at 9 am the next day, about 21 hours total.

Are meals included in a day cruise?
Yes: usually a welcome drink, lunch, and evening tea with snacks. Dinner and breakfast come only with the overnight package.

Can I see the sunset on a day cruise?
Not on the boat. Houseboats anchor by evening, so the day cruise ends around 5 pm, before dusk. Watch the sunset from the Alleppey beach or a lakeside resort instead, or book the overnight.
