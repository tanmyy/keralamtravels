How to Reach Munnar from Kochi Airport

The direct way to reach Munnar from Kochi Airport (COK) is by taxi: about 110 to 115 km via NH 85, taking 3.5 to 4.5 hours, with a sedan from Rs 3,300 on one operator tariff checked for this guide. The cheapest way is a KSRTC bus from Aluva, from Rs 188 per person, after a short transfer from the airport. Munnar has no airport and no railway station, so every route ends on the same hill road.

Last updated: October 2026

Quick comparison: taxi vs bus from Kochi Airport to Munnar

Cochin International Airport at Nedumbassery is the nearest airport to Munnar. Sources differ slightly on distance because Munnar is an area, not one point: a Munnar resort guide puts the airport at about 110 km, while a local taxi operator lists about 115 km to Munnar town and about 135 km to outlying Chinnakanal, where several large resorts sit. Budget for the longer figure if your stay is outside town.

Taxi from Kochi Airport to Munnar: fares and booking checks

A taxi is the only single-ride option. You walk out of arrivals, meet your driver, and the next stop is your hotel. The published one-way tariff of one Munnar-based operator, checked in October 2026, is:

Treat that table as a benchmark, not a fixed price. Another Kochi operator lists a sedan on this route at Rs 4,960 and an Innova Crysta at Rs 7,820, so quotes vary a lot by operator and by what is included. Before you pay, confirm four things in writing: whether tolls, parking, and driver charges are included, whether the price covers a drop in Munnar town or at an outlying resort, the charge per extra kilometre, and whether any waiting charge applies if your flight is delayed. Our separate comparison of Kochi to Munnar taxi fare vs bus cost explains why one-way hill drops cost more per kilometre than city rides.

To book the taxi well, follow these steps:

1. Book at least a day ahead in peak season, and share your flight number so the driver can track a delay.

2. Agree the exact drop point by hotel name, not just Munnar, because Chinnakanal and Pallivasal add distance.

3. Ask for an experienced hill driver and a daylight departure where possible. The ghat section is winding, and first-time visitors often prefer it in daylight.

4. Keep the driver phone number and your hotel number saved offline. Mobile coverage thins out on the climb.

Bus from Kochi Airport to Munnar via Aluva, step by step

There is no direct bus from the airport terminal to Munnar, so the bus route is a two-step journey. Aluva is the practical hub: its KSRTC stand sits by the railway and metro stations and has the Munnar services closest to the airport.

1. Leave the airport and travel to Aluva by local taxi, auto, or airport feeder service. Allow time for this hop and for finding the KSRTC stand at Aluva.

2. At Aluva, take a KSRTC bus towards Munnar. A KSRTC listing for Aluva to Munnar shows fares starting at Rs 188 per person, a shortest journey of about 3 hours 40 minutes, and a first departure listed at 6:10 am. Services and times change, so check the KSRTC booking site or the depot board on the day rather than planning around one fixed time.

3. Ride via Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam, and Adimali to the Munnar bus stand, then take a short auto or taxi ride to your hotel if it is outside town.

The bus is genuinely cheap for one person, and the climb is scenic, but it is the hardest option with large suitcases, small children, or a late flight. If you land in the evening, a taxi or a night in Kochi followed by a morning bus is usually the saner plan. If you are splitting your trip, our Kochi airport to Fort Kochi taxi fare guide covers the city side.

The NH 85 route and how long it really takes

Almost every driver uses NH 85: airport and Aluva, then Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam, Adimali, and Munnar. The first part is a normal highway run. After Neriamangalam the road climbs through forest and tea country, with waterfalls such as Cheeyappara and Valara beside the road, and speed drops sharply. That is why about 110 km takes as long as a much longer plains drive.

One operator estimates 3 hours 30 minutes for the drive and advises allowing a further 30 to 60 minutes for traffic delays. Plan on 3.5 to 4.5 hours in total, longer in heavy rain, during holiday traffic, or for a drop beyond Munnar town. If you get motion sick, sit in the front, avoid a heavy meal before the climb, and ask for short photo stops rather than pushing through.

Why the train does not help here

Munnar has no railway station, and the nearest stations, Aluva and Ernakulam, still leave you with the same 3.5 to 4.5 hour road climb. Coming from the airport, going to a station adds a change without saving money or time. Trains only make sense if you are arriving into Kerala by rail in the first place, not as part of an airport transfer.

Which option should you pick?

Pick the taxi if you are two or more people, carrying luggage, travelling with children or older parents, or landing after mid-afternoon. Split four ways, a Rs 3,300 sedan is about Rs 825 per person for a direct drop, which is strong value against a full day of connections. Pick the bus if you are solo, travelling light, landing in the morning, and happy to treat the Aluva change as part of the trip. FAQ

How far is Munnar from Kochi Airport?

About 110 to 115 km by road to Munnar town, depending on the source and your exact drop point. Outlying resort areas such as Chinnakanal are listed at about 135 km from the airport.

How long does it take from Kochi Airport to Munnar?

About 3.5 to 4.5 hours by taxi. By bus via Aluva, allow about 4.5 to 5.5 hours in total, including the airport to Aluva transfer, waiting time, and a bus journey of about 3 hours 40 minutes or more.

How much is a taxi from Kochi Airport to Munnar?

On one operator tariff checked in October 2026, Rs 3,300 for a Swift Dzire sedan, Rs 4,800 for an Innova, and Rs 5,800 for an Innova Crysta, for Munnar town. Other operators quote higher, so confirm inclusions before you book.

Is there a direct bus from Kochi Airport to Munnar?

No direct bus from the terminal was found for this guide. The standard bus route is airport to Aluva, then a KSRTC bus from Aluva to Munnar, with fares for that leg starting at Rs 188 per person on the listing checked.

Can I reach Munnar the same evening if my flight lands late?

By pre-booked taxi, yes, but you will climb the ghat road in the dark and arrive late. Many travellers prefer a night near the airport or in Kochi and an early start, so the scenic half of the drive happens in daylight.

What is the cheapest way to reach Munnar from Kochi Airport?

The KSRTC bus via Aluva. The Aluva to Munnar fare starts at Rs 188 per person on the listing checked, plus whatever your short airport to Aluva transfer costs.

Honest caveat: fares checked in October 2026 from operator tariffs and a KSRTC listing. Taxi quotes vary by operator, season, and drop point, and bus times change, so confirm both before you travel.
