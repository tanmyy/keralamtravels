Kochi to Trivandrum: train vs flight vs car cost and time

Take the train. Kochi to Trivandrum is about 212 km by road and 215 km by rail, and 21 trains run every day, so the train is both the cheapest and usually the fastest door-to-door option. The Vande Bharat covers the run in 3 hours 22 minutes, and tickets start at Rs 100 in second sitting, with AC classes listed from Rs 355 to Rs 1,190. Flights look faster at 55 minutes, but after transfers and check-in they take about as long and cost Rs 4,300 to Rs 6,000. A private cab is Rs 7,319 to Rs 10,165 all-in for the 5.5-hour drive, and it earns its price for groups or heavy luggage.

By train: cheapest, and usually fastest door to door

This is one of the busiest rail corridors in Kerala: 21 daily trains run from Kochi (Ernakulam Junction or Ernakulam Town) to Thiruvananthapuram Central, covering about 215 km. Stations sit in the city centre at both ends, so there is no airport-style transfer time at either side, and no check-in queue.

The fastest options are the two Vande Bharat runs. Train 20631 leaves Ernakulam Junction at 11:43 and reaches Trivandrum Central at 15:05, in 3 hours 22 minutes, running every day except Wednesday. Train 20633 leaves Ernakulam Town at 19:15 and reaches at 22:40, in 3 hours 25 minutes. The Jan Shatabdi 12075 is the evening workhorse for everyone else: 17:25 to 21:25, four hours flat, with chair-car seating.

Minimum listed fares on this route, from ixigo's fare table checked in October 2026, are Rs 100 in second sitting, Rs 150 in sleeper, Rs 355 in chair car, Rs 435 in 3AC, Rs 725 in 2AC and Rs 1,190 in first AC. Vande Bharat fares are set separately at a premium over ordinary express fares, so check the current fare on IRCTC when you book. On weekends and holidays, the Vande Bharat and the Jan Shatabdi sell out first, so book a few days ahead; the slow expresses almost always have sleeper seats left.

One habit that pays: the 3h22m daytime Vande Bharat arrives in Trivandrum at 15:05, which is too late for a full afternoon of sightseeing but perfect if your hotel check-in is 2 pm anyway. If you want a full day in the city, take an early express and you are in by 9:40 am for Rs 100. Once you arrive, our Trivandrum 2 day itinerary covers how to use the time.

By flight: 55 minutes in the air, longer door to door

IndiGo flies Kochi (COK) to Trivandrum (TRV) direct in 55 minutes, and Air India lists the air distance at 190 km. Direct departures cluster around mid-morning and early evening. It is the only transport mode where the clock on the ticket is not the clock on your trip.

Booked ahead, direct one-way fares run about Rs 4,270 to Rs 6,006: fare sites in October 2026 showed a low of Rs 4,270 one way, ixigo's cheapest direct at Rs 6,006 on IndiGo, and the cheapest day of the coming week at Rs 5,976. Booked last minute, the same seat can list at Rs 17,675. Then add the transfers: getting from Kochi city to the airport is Rs 1,340 to Rs 1,500 by prepaid taxi or about Rs 100 by bus, per our own Kochi airport taxi fare guide, and you need to be at the airport 60 to 90 minutes before departure.

Add it up and the flight's real cost is Rs 5,500 to Rs 8,000 all-in for one person, and the real time is 3 to 4 hours door to door, barely better than the Vande Bharat's 3h22m at a fraction of the price. The flight earns its keep only when your schedule lines up exactly with one of the direct departures and you can skip the transfers, for instance if you are landing at Kochi airport from another flight and connecting onward to Trivandrum.

By car or taxi: door-to-door freedom at a premium

The road route is NH66, the coastal highway, running about 212 km through Kollam and Alappuzha. Expect 5 to 5.5 hours: one drop-taxi operator quotes 211.99 km and 5 hours 26 minutes, and a Trivandrum-based operator quotes 210 km and 5 to 5.5 hours. NH66 is a good four-lane road with frequent tolls, so pick an all-inclusive fare that bundles them. The scenic alternative, MC Road through the hinterland, takes around 7 hours and suits travellers who want the drive itself to be the point.

One-way drop-taxi fares checked in October 2026 start at Rs 7,319 for a sedan, Rs 8,132 for an SUV and Rs 10,165 for an Innova, all-inclusive with tolls, driver allowance and GST. From Kochi airport specifically, Blue Bird Travels lists Rs 9,430 for a Swift DZire and Rs 12,230 for an Innova, including fuel, toll, parking and driver allowance. Note that aggregator fares from different cities vary: the general Kochi to Trivandrum one-way sedan fare can list lower on some sites before tolls and taxes are added, so always confirm whether the quote is all-in before you book.

Do the per-person math before dismissing the taxi. Four people in a Rs 7,319 sedan pay about Rs 1,830 each, which is in the same territory as two 2AC train tickets per person with zero transfers, and you leave when you want and stop at Kollam or Alappuzha on the way. If you would rather drive yourself, our guide to self-drive car rental in Kerala covers daily rates, deposits and licence rules.

Which should you pick?

For most travellers the answer is the train, and specifically the Vande Bharat if the timings work: 3h22m, city centre to city centre, no check-in, at a fare that starts at Rs 100 in the cheapest class. Budget solo travellers should look at the early-morning expresses, 4h20m for Rs 100, which is hard to argue with.

Pick the flight when a direct departure lines up with your schedule and the fare is booked ahead under Rs 6,000, or when you are connecting through Kochi airport anyway. At last-minute prices around Rs 17,000, the flight is never worth it on this route: the Vande Bharat beats it on total time and costs less than a tenth.

Pick the private taxi when you are 3 or 4 people splitting the fare, carrying real luggage, travelling with elders or children, or planning stops at Kollam, Alappuzha or Varkala on the way. A family of four pays roughly train money per head and gets door-to-door service. Our comparison of Kochi to Alleppey by taxi, train and bus follows the same logic on the shorter leg up the coast.

FAQ

Is there a direct flight from Kochi to Trivandrum?

Yes. IndiGo flies direct from Kochi (COK) to Trivandrum (TRV) in 55 minutes. Fares booked ahead start around Rs 4,270 one way and typically list at Rs 6,000 to Rs 6,600 near the travel date, rising toward Rs 17,675 at the last minute, based on fare listings checked in October 2026.

How long does the Vande Bharat take from Kochi to Trivandrum?

3 hours 22 minutes on train 20631 (Ernakulam Junction 11:43 to Trivandrum Central 15:05, not running Wednesday), or 3 hours 25 minutes on train 20633 (Ernakulam Town 19:15 to Trivandrum Central 22:40). Both are the fastest rail options on the route.

What is the taxi fare from Kochi to Trivandrum?

One-way drop-taxi fares checked in October 2026 start at Rs 7,319 for a sedan, Rs 8,132 for an SUV and Rs 10,165 for an Innova, all-inclusive with tolls, driver allowance and GST. From Kochi airport, Blue Bird Travels lists Rs 9,430 for a Swift DZire and Rs 12,230 for an Innova.

Is the train or flight better from Kochi to Trivandrum?

The train, for almost everyone. It is city centre to city centre with no check-in, the fastest run is 3h22m, and fares start at Rs 100. The flight is 55 minutes in the air but 3 to 4 hours door to door after transfers and reporting time, at roughly 5 to 10 times the price.

Can I do Kochi to Trivandrum as a day trip?

Yes. With trains running through the day in both directions and the fastest run at 3h22m, you can leave Kochi on an early express and be back by night. The 11:43 Vande Bharat down and an evening return is the most comfortable same-day pattern, but book the return seat in advance.

Honest caveat: flight fares on this route swing hard with demand, a seat listed at Rs 5,976 today can be Rs 17,675 tomorrow, so the flight figures here are ranges from October 2026 listings, not quotes. Train fares and taxi tariffs are more stable, but confirm the total before you pay.
