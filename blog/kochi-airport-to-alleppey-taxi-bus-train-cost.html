Kochi Airport to Alleppey: Taxi, Bus and Train Options with Cost

Take a taxi if you want the simplest trip: Cochin International Airport (CIAL) at Nedumbassery to Alleppey town is 82 to 88 km by road, a sedan costs about Rs 2,200 to Rs 3,170 one way, and the drive takes 2 to 2.5 hours. The budget route costs about Rs 305 in total, a feeder bus to Aluva plus a train to Alappuzha, but it takes about 2 hours 50 minutes of travel time plus waiting, and there is no direct bus from the airport to Alleppey.

At a glance: the three ways from the airport

The road from CIAL runs south through Kalamassery and Edappally, then down NH 66 through Aroor and Cherthala into Alappuzha town. That is why the distance is quoted as 82 km by one operator and 88 km by another: the exact drop point matters, and so does the route taken around Kochi traffic and the Aroor to Thuravoor highway works. Budget 2 to 2.5 hours for the drive, and up to 3 hours in peak traffic.

The key difference from the city-centre post is the start point. Kochi city to Alleppey is about 55 to 60 km, but the airport sits north of the city, so the airport run adds roughly 25 km and the drive through Kochi. Do not use a city fare for an airport pickup, and do not use an airport fare for a city pickup.

Option 1: Taxi from Kochi airport to Alleppey

This is the only door-to-door option, and the only one that drops you directly at a houseboat jetty such as Punnamada or Finishing Point, or at a Marari Beach resort on the way. Taxis are available 24 hours a day at the airport, and you prepay at the counter or kiosk in the terminal before you go to the taxi rank. Pre-booking gives you a named driver waiting in arrivals and a fixed quote; a walk-up prepaid taxi gives you the next car in the queue.

Why the sedan range is wide: one operator quotes Rs 2,200 to Rs 2,500 as an all-inclusive flat fare covering tolls and airport parking, while another listing puts the same run at about Rs 3,170 and notes that tolls can be added to the fare. Before you pay, ask one question and get the answer in writing: does this quote include tolls and airport parking, or are they extra? That single answer explains most fare arguments on this route.

For a group, the taxi math flips the budget ranking. A sedan at Rs 2,500 split four ways is Rs 625 per person, and an Ertiga at Rs 3,300 split six ways is Rs 550 per person. At that point the taxi is not much more than the bus and train combo, and it saves the transfers with luggage. For a solo traveller, the bus and train combo stays far cheaper.

Option 2: Bus and train from Kochi airport to Alleppey, step by step

There is no direct bus from the airport to Alleppey. The budget route is two legs, and both legs are cheap. Work through it in this order:

  1. Take the feeder bus from the airport bus stand to Aluva station. This takes about 30 minutes and costs about Rs 80. Buses run roughly every 20 to 40 minutes, and the airport bus service runs about 06:45 to 23:15.
  2. At Aluva, buy a train ticket to Alappuzha (station code ALLP). The train leg takes 1 hour 40 minutes to 2 hours 40 minutes depending on the service, and costs about Rs 255 on the combined-trip listing, giving a total of about Rs 305 for the whole journey.
  3. At Alappuzha station, take an auto to your hotel or jetty. The station is about 4 km from Finishing Point jetty, so this last leg is short, but it is not included in the Rs 305 figure, so keep small change ready.

Allow for waiting, not just travel time. The combined listing gives 170 minutes of travel, but trains do not leave every few minutes, and the service thins out at night. If you land late, check the next Aluva to Alappuzha train before you leave the airport, and have the taxi as a backup. Heavy suitcases also change the calculation: the feeder bus and train have luggage space, but two large bags plus a transfer at Aluva is a different trip from one backpack.

Option 3: Bus-only via Kochi city, as a fallback

If the Aluva train timing does not work, head into Kochi city first, then take a KSRTC bus to Alappuzha. The city leg is about 55 km from the Vyttila hub and buses run frequently, but fares vary by service type, so check the fare at the counter rather than relying on a guessed total here.

A related city option is worth knowing if you are staying in Kochi first: Kochi airport to Fort Kochi is a much shorter run, about 40 km, with prepaid taxi fares of about Rs 1,340 to Rs 1,500 and a bus for about Rs 100. That is a different trip from the airport to Alleppey, and mixing the two fares up is the most common budgeting mistake on this route.

Which option fits your arrival?

Houseboat boarding is the deciding factor for many arrivals. Alleppey houseboats typically board between 12 noon and 1 pm, so a morning flight plus a taxi gets you to the jetty in good time, while a morning flight plus the bus and train combo is tight if the train connection is slow. If your flight lands after lunch, you have usually missed that day's boarding anyway, so the cheaper route costs you nothing in cruise time.

Night arrivals point the same way. Taxis run 24 hours a day, while the airport bus service ends at about 23:15 and trains thin out at night. A family with children, an elderly traveller, or anyone with two large suitcases should also default to the taxi, even in daytime, because the saving on the bus and train route is spent on transfers and hassle.

Marari Beach sits about 14 km before Alleppey town and about 72 to 74 km from the airport, so a taxi there is slightly shorter than a taxi to town. By bus and train, Marari is awkward, since you would still need a taxi back north from Alappuzha town.

Return trip: Alleppey to Kochi airport

For the return, one operator advises leaving Alleppey town at least 5 hours before a domestic flight and 6 hours before an international flight: 2 to 2.5 hours of driving, plus check-in, security, and a buffer for NH 66 traffic. If your flight is in the morning, book the taxi the night before and confirm the pickup time in writing.

Honest caveat: taxi fares on this route are operator quotes, not a government tariff, so two reputable listings can differ by hundreds of rupees for the same sedan. Treat Rs 2,200 to Rs 3,170 as the realistic sedan range, confirm whether tolls and parking are included, and confirm the exact jetty or hotel name, not just "Alleppey", before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a direct bus from Kochi airport to Alleppey? No. There is no direct public bus on this route. The budget option is a feeder bus to Aluva plus a train to Alappuzha, or a bus via Kochi city.

How far is Kochi airport from Alleppey? About 82 to 88 km by road, depending on the drop point and the route. The drive takes 2 to 2.5 hours in normal traffic.

How much is a taxi from Kochi airport to Alleppey? A sedan costs about Rs 2,200 to Rs 3,170 one way, an Ertiga about Rs 3,000 to Rs 3,300, and an Innova Crysta about Rs 4,000 to Rs 4,500, per operator tariffs checked in October 2026.

What is the cheapest way from Kochi airport to Alleppey? The feeder bus to Aluva (about Rs 80) plus a train to Alappuzha (about Rs 255), about Rs 305 in total, plus an auto from Alappuzha station to your hotel or jetty.

Can I reach a houseboat the same day I land? Yes, if you land in the morning and take a taxi, since houseboats board around 12 noon to 1 pm. Read the houseboat booking checklist before you pay, and tell the taxi driver your exact jetty name.
