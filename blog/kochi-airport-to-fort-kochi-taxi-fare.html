Kochi Airport to Fort Kochi Taxi Fare

The prepaid taxi from Cochin International Airport (COK) to Fort Kochi costs about Rs 1,340 to Rs 1,500 for a standard car. The airport charges by the kilometre, not a flat Fort Kochi rate, and the ride is about 37 km. App cabs usually land in a similar range, while the direct bus costs about Rs 100 per person.

Last updated: October 2026

Quick answer: prepaid, app cab, or bus

Fort Kochi is about 37 km from the airport by road. In light traffic it can be done in under an hour, but plan for 1 to 1.5 hours normally, and longer in weekday peak traffic through Edappally and Ernakulam.

Those prepaid figures are a calculation, not a promise. A longer route, a drop deep inside Fort Kochi, or a larger car moves the total. If the counter quote is close to the table above, it matches the official rate.

How the official prepaid fare is worked out

Cochin International Airport Ltd (CIAL) says prepaid taxis run round the clock to destinations in and around Cochin, with computerised counters in both the domestic and international terminals. The system is run through a cooperative society controlled by CIAL. The tariff on the airport site, revised with effect from 5 May 2022, is:

The same airport page lists a minimum billing distance of 15 km and a sanitisation charge of Rs 35.70 including taxes. Fare trackers checked in October 2026 put this taxi at about Rs 1,500 for 37.1 km, which fits the official calculation once extra charges are added. Recent traveller reports put a prepaid ride at about Rs 1,500 and an app cab return at about Rs 1,350, so plan on Rs 1,400 to Rs 1,500 for a car.

How to take the prepaid taxi, step by step

1. Stay inside the terminal after baggage claim and look for the prepaid taxi counter. There is one in each terminal. Do not agree a fare with touts in the arrivals hall first.

2. Tell the counter your exact drop point in Fort Kochi, by hotel or landmark name, and the car size you need. Solo travellers and couples fit the ordinary car. Families should ask for the 6-seater rate rather than squeezing into a sedan.

3. Pay at the counter and keep the receipt. The receipt is your proof of the fixed fare. You should not need to renegotiate with the driver.

4. Follow the signs to the prepaid pickup area outside, show the receipt, and note the car number before you leave. Keep small notes handy for any toll or parking charge that the counter says is not included, and ask at the counter what is included before you pay.

5. Follow the route on your phone if you like. The usual road runs via Aluva and Edappally, then Ernakulam toward Fort Kochi. Traffic decides whether it takes 55 or 90 minutes.

App cabs: when they beat the counter, and when they do not

Uber and Ola both operate in Kochi, including at the airport, and they work 24 hours. The advantage is price transparency in the app and no counter queue. The disadvantage is surge pricing when several flights land together, and a longer walk to the app pickup point with luggage.

Practical rule: check the app while you wait for bags. If the quote is below about Rs 1,300 and a car is close, take it. If it is surging toward Rs 1,600 or the wait is long, the prepaid counter is calmer and fixed. At night, the counter also avoids hunting for a car in the car park.

The bus option for about Rs 100

KSRTC low-floor air-conditioned buses have long run directly between the airport and Fort Kochi, and travellers report a fare of about Rs 100 per person with the ticket bought on the bus. It is easily the cheapest way to do this journey, and for a solo traveller with a backpack it can save over Rs 1,200 compared with a car.

The trade-offs are real. Buses run to a timetable that changes, so check current departures at the airport information desk on landing rather than relying on an old list online. The ride is slower, luggage space is limited, and late at night there may be no bus for hours, when the prepaid taxi is the fallback. If you miss a direct bus, go via Ernakulam and change to a local bus or ferry, which is cheap but adds a change with luggage.

Which option should you pick?

Pick prepaid if this is your first visit, you land at night, or you have checked bags: the fixed fare removes negotiation and surge watching. Pick an app cab if you travel light and its quote is lower. Pick the bus if you are solo, in daylight, and saving money matters more than saving 30 to 40 minutes. Have your Fort Kochi address in writing: the lanes there have similar names.

FAQ

How far is Fort Kochi from Kochi airport?

About 37 to 40 km depending on the exact drop point and route taken. Fare trackers use 37.1 km for the airport to Fort Kochi calculation. Allow 1 to 1.5 hours by road in normal traffic.

Is there a flat prepaid fare to Fort Kochi?

No flat destination fare is published on the airport site. CIAL publishes a per-kilometre tariff (Rs 36 for an ordinary car, revised in May 2022), and the counter works your fare out from the distance. For Fort Kochi that comes to roughly Rs 1,340 to Rs 1,500.

Are prepaid taxis available at night?

Yes. The airport says prepaid taxis are available round the clock, with counters in both terminals. That makes the prepaid counter the most reliable option for late-night and early-morning arrivals, when buses are not running and app cab supply is thin.

Is Uber cheaper than the prepaid taxi from Kochi airport?

Sometimes. In normal demand an app cab can come in a little below the counter, around Rs 1,100 to Rs 1,350 in recent reports. When flights bunch together and surge applies, the app can cost more than the fixed prepaid fare. Check both before you commit.

Can I take an auto-rickshaw from the airport to Fort Kochi?

Not as a pickup from inside the airport. Autos can drop passengers at the airport but cannot wait there to pick up arrivals, so do not plan on an auto for the full 37 km. Take a prepaid taxi, app cab, or bus from the airport, and use autos for short hops inside Fort Kochi once you arrive.

Honest caveat: prepaid figures here are calculated from the official CIAL per-kilometre tariff published on the airport site (revised 5 May 2022) for a 37.1 km ride, checked in October 2026. The counter quote on the day is the fare you pay. App cab fares are dynamic, private operators quote their own prices, and bus timings change, so confirm all three on arrival.
